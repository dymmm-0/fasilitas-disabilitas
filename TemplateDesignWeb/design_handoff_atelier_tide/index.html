How we work

Four projects a year. One studio, two cities.

A practice run from a former boatyard in Lisbon and a small apartment above a bakery in Naples, by eight architects who insist on detailing every corner themselves.

We accept commissions on a rolling basis through May 31 each year — four per cycle, by partner preference. We do not enter open competitions. We do not, with apologies, do "concept" work.

i.One partner leads each project from brief to last screw.
ii.We build, when possible, within 200 km of the site.
iii.Two materials per project. Three is a compromise.
iv.We don't publish renderings — only photographs, of the built thing.
